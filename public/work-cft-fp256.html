IWhat it is

A math coprocessor that gets the same answer everywhere (cft-fp256 77b8440 README.md:3). It does IEEE 754 arithmetic at four precisions - 32, 64, 128 and 256 bits - and guarantees that the same inputs give the same bits whether the work runs on a laptop CPU, in a browser tab, on a microcontroller, or on the FPGA card it was designed for (cft-fp256 77b8440 README.md:5).

IIWhat it is not

Raw fp32 and fp64 throughput (cft-fp256 77b8440 README.md:64). Every CPU and GPU on the market serves those formats at clocks and lane counts an FPGA fabric will not match, and nothing here pretends otherwise (cft-fp256 77b8440 README.md:64). The tile earns its place at the precisions commodity hardware does not offer, and on the guarantee that the answer does not move (cft-fp256 77b8440 README.md:70).

IIIThe contract

Named levels, quoted from CONFORMANCE.md: “Level A - IEEE 754-2019 in radix 2 (cft-fp256 77b8440 CONFORMANCE.md:15)” is the standard itself; “Level B - the cft-fp256 profile (cft-fp256 77b8440 CONFORMANCE.md:21)” is that plus every choice the standard leaves open, fixed to one answer. The relationship runs one way, in the document's own words: “An implementation conforming to the cft-fp256 profile conforms to (cft-fp256 77b8440 CONFORMANCE.md:26) IEEE 754-2019 in radix 2 for the operations the profile covers (cft-fp256 77b8440 CONFORMANCE.md:27).”

The README states the promise directly: “That guarantee is the product (cft-fp256 77b8440 README.md:10).” The hardware only makes it faster (cft-fp256 77b8440 README.md:10).

VITest it

  1. replays the published conformance vectors in front of you (cft-fp256 77b8440 README.md:30) — a browser tab, nothing installed.
  2. make golden: The golden model's self-tests (cft-fp256 77b8440 README.md:254) (needs only Python and, optionally, mpmath).
  3. the card: compares a device against the software backend bit for bit across the elementwise operations (cft-fp256 77b8440 CONFORMANCE.md:246), which needs the FPGA and its toolchain.

VIIProve it wrong

Cheapest first; the site-wide rules of engagement are stated once, on the Corrections page — not yet built, so this dossier does not link it.

  1. Open the web page above and hand it your own vector file: “Drop a vector file on it and it scores itself (cft-fp256 77b8440 README.md:34).” Anything less than a perfect score is a disproof.
  2. make golden locally, against the same vector sets published in the repository's vectors/SHA256SUMS (cft-fp256 77b8440).
  3. Replay the identity protocol yourself in another language and compare its checksum line against the ones docs/COMPATIBILITY.md (cft-fp256 77b8440) records; a differing hex value is a disproof.
  4. Rebuild host/tools/mpfr_check.c and widen its sweep past the campaign's own cases: the independent oracle is only as good as the cases put to it.
