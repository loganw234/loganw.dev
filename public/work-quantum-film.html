IWhat it is

Film stocks whose crystals are laid by quantum circuits, and a fixer that makes every print of a roll the same, bit for bit, on every machine (Quantum-Film 3a73823 README.md:3).

IIWhat it is not

  • Not a quantum advantage. At these sizes both quantum laws are classically simulable. That is exactly what makes them checkable (Quantum-Film 3a73823 README.md:269)
  • Not deterministic at the shot. A roll from a device is unique (Quantum-Film 3a73823 README.md:273)
  • The comparison measures; it does not gate. It says how far each device is from the law and from the twin, not whether a device "passed" (Quantum-Film 3a73823 docs/HARDWARE-RESULTS.md:195)
  • Not a model of any real film's grain. (Quantum-Film 3a73823 README.md:278)

IIIThe contract

“The authority is quantum_film/golden: pure Python and mpmath at 256 bits, with exact counter-based uniforms (SHA-256) (Quantum-Film 3a73823 README.md:291) It imports nothing it could share with what it judges (Quantum-Film 3a73823 README.md:292); a test holds that mechanically (Quantum-Film 3a73823 README.md:293).” The circuits, the float paths and Atlas's results are all scored against it (Quantum-Film 3a73823 README.md:293).

VIIProve it wrong

Cheapest first; the site-wide rules of engagement are stated once, on the Corrections page.

  1. Look at the first prints yourself and compare the structure-factor claim against what the eye can actually see in the published images — free.
  2. Re-make a table of docs/HARDWARE-RESULTS.md from the committed records, and read whether any cell differs. “Every table below is quantum_film/compare.py's own, made from the records committed under docs/records/2026-10-05/ibm/, and every number in the prose is computed from them (Quantum-Film 3a73823 docs/HARDWARE-RESULTS.md:3)”: python -m quantum_film.compare table --prediction <a prediction> docs/records/2026-10-05/ibm/<device>/run (Quantum-Film 3a73823 docs/HARDWARE-RESULTS.md:207).
  3. make verify-quick on a clean clone, and read whether the README's “The negative control, and its twin (Quantum-Film 3a73823 README.md:303)” still catch a moved crystal.
  4. Feed the fixer a record with one crystal moved yourself, by hand, and confirm it is refused for its digest and for no other reason.
  5. Repeat the same Atlas job and check whether the bytes really differ: “Identical requests to Atlas returned different bytes wherever sampling was involved (Quantum-Film 3a73823 README.md:263),” which needs an Atlas account this dossier does not supply.
