Quantum-Film — dossier
======================

I. What it is

Film stocks whose crystals are laid by quantum circuits, and a fixer that makes every print of a roll the same, bit for bit, on every machine [fact 1877].

II. What it is not

- Not a quantum advantage. At these sizes both quantum laws are classically simulable. That is exactly what makes them checkable [fact 1878]
- Not deterministic at the shot. A roll from a device is unique [fact 1879]
- The comparison measures; it does not gate. It says how far each device is from the law and from the twin, not whether a device "passed" [fact 1880]
- Not a model of any real film's grain. [fact 1881]

III. The contract

“The authority is quantum_film/golden: pure Python and mpmath at 256 bits, with exact counter-based uniforms (SHA-256) [fact 1882] It imports nothing it could share with what it judges [fact 1883]; a test holds that mechanically [fact 1884].” The circuits, the float paths and Atlas's results are all scored against it [fact 1885].

VII. Prove it wrong

Cheapest first; the site-wide rules of engagement are stated once, on the Corrections page.

- Look at the first prints yourself and compare the structure-factor claim against what the eye can actually see in the published images — free.
- Re-make a table of docs/HARDWARE-RESULTS.md from the committed records, and read whether any cell differs. “Every table below is quantum_film/compare.py's own, made from the records committed under docs/records/2026-10-05/ibm/, and every number in the prose is computed from them [fact 1910]”: python -m quantum_film.compare table --prediction <a prediction> docs/records/2026-10-05/ibm/<device>/run [fact 1911].
- make verify-quick on a clean clone, and read whether the README's “The negative control, and its twin [fact 1912]” still catch a moved crystal.
- Feed the fixer a record with one crystal moved yourself, by hand, and confirm it is refused for its digest and for no other reason.
- Repeat the same Atlas job and check whether the bytes really differ: “Identical requests to Atlas returned different bytes wherever sampling was involved [fact 1913],” which needs an Atlas account this dossier does not supply.
