Quantum-Film — dossier
======================

I. What it is

Film stocks whose crystals are laid by quantum circuits, and a fixer that makes every print of a roll the same, bit for bit, on every machine [fact 1533].

II. What it is not

- Not a quantum advantage. At these sizes both quantum laws are classically simulable. That is exactly what makes them checkable [fact 1534]
- Not deterministic at the shot. A roll from a device is unique [fact 1535]
- Not on hardware yet. [fact 1536]
- Not a model of any real film's grain. [fact 1537]

III. The contract

“The authority is quantum_film/golden: pure Python and mpmath at 256 bits, with exact counter-based uniforms (SHA-256) [fact 1538] It imports nothing it could share with what it judges [fact 1539]; a test holds that mechanically [fact 1540].” The circuits, the float paths and Atlas's results are all scored against it [fact 1541].

VII. Prove it wrong

Cheapest first; the site-wide rules of engagement are stated once, on the Corrections page — not yet built, so this dossier does not link it.

- Look at the first prints yourself and compare the structure-factor claim against what the eye can actually see in the published images — free.
- make verify-quick on a clean clone, and read whether The negative control, and its twin [fact 1554] still catches a moved crystal.
- Feed the fixer a record with one crystal moved yourself, by hand, and confirm it is refused for its digest and for no other reason.
- Repeat the same Atlas job and check whether the bytes really differ: “Identical requests to Atlas returned different bytes wherever sampling was involved [fact 1555],” which needs an Atlas account this dossier does not supply.
