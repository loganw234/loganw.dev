Bit-identical arithmetic: a picture, a language for it, a coprocessor that will not drift, and what got built once it existed.

IThe account

It starts with a picture, and ends with a promise that the picture's numbers hold wherever they run. A WebGL2 point-cloud atlas (PrettyCloud 5161daa README.md:3). Its shapes wanted a language of their own: A language for platonography (atlas-engine 867c57a README.md:3) re-running Sixty-eight of sixty-eight, one hash (atlas-engine 867c57a README.md:176) against the plates it draws.

The language needed arithmetic that could not drift between machines, which is what cft-fp256 is: A math coprocessor that gets the same answer everywhere (cft-fp256 77b8440 README.md:3) Its ABI is 0.15 (cft-fp256 77b8440 host/include/cft.h:111) on main, checked by make verify-quick (~20 min, 28 of 41 stages (cft-fp256 77b8440 CLAUDE.md:9)).

Built on it: REBOUND's IAS15 integrator with its arithmetic routed through libcft (cft-rebound 039e3c3 README.md:3): At binary64 it is REBOUND's own IAS15 bit for bit (cft-rebound 039e3c3 README.md:22). The method itself was distilled out of this work: cft-rebound's own round history says it was lifted into a standalone repository (cft-rebound 039e3c3 docs/PARCEL-ROUNDS.md:13: “lifted into a standalone repository”), ParcelRound, A method for splitting one body of work across several coding agents at once (ParcelRound 8767e23 README.md:3); and cft-fp256's case study in HonestFramework, A way to lay out a project so that an AI can write nearly all of it and no claim about it ever rests on the AI's judgement (HonestFramework 65447fd README.md:3).

binary-sites is private (GitHub snapshot 2026-09-29, private for now) for now: Four sites, one per IEEE 754 binary interchange format (binary-sites dacdcb8 README.md:5, private for now), binary32.com · binary64.com · binary128.com · binary256.com (binary-sites dacdcb8 README.md:3, private for now).

nextpnr-xilinx is a fork, kept for one FPGA target a fork of openXC7's router, which does not converge on the tile (cft-fp256 77b8440 docs/README.md:118: “openXC7's router does not converge on the tile, what to change in it and in what order, and why a fork of it (loganw234/nextpnr-xilinx, dense) starts at the pinned 0.9.6”). The map places it on 2026-09-23 (GitHub snapshot 2026-09-29: created (a fork - dated by GitHub's record of when the fork itself was made, in UTC, never by a commit, since its earliest commit belongs to the project it forked)) — read as when GitHub made the fork itself, never a commit: a fork's own commits are mostly its parent's, and this one's earliest, 2018-05-23 (GitHub snapshot 2026-09-29), is upstream's, from before this project existed.
