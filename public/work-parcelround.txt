ParcelRound — dossier
=====================

I. What it is

A method for splitting one body of work across several coding agents at once, without the pieces failing to meet [fact 1381].

II. What it is not

Under “Is this for you? [fact 1382]”: “Don't” for a single task, for exploratory work where the split isn't obvious yet, or where the pieces can't be tested separately [fact 1383]. Two agents on a two-way split is usually slower than doing it yourself, because the brief costs more than the work [fact 1384].

III. The contract

Under “The one failure mode [fact 1385]”: the work between the parcels belongs to nobody, and it is invisible because every parcel's own gate is green [fact 1386].

IV. Verified

No docs/VALIDATION.md ledger and no workflow: the method has no build of its own to gate. What its case studies record instead is a measurement of the method: 

- The round CASE-STUDY.md measures returned “twelve corrections from five parcels — every single parcel corrected its brief [fact 1387]”, on the strength of one line: “Report anything you found that this brief got wrong [fact 1388].”

V. Kept failures

The fix that finally held for the lead was mechanical: write the entry with a placeholder and let the append command substitute date [fact 1390] The method's own rule now: “stamps are substituted, not typed [fact 1391]” Recorded by commit f2a0483 [fact 1392].

Recorded by commit 42e76cc [fact 1397].

VI. Test it

- Read CASE-STUDY.md's worked example yourself and check its timeline is internally consistent — free, and the whole argument for the method rests on it.
- Compare templates/brief.md and templates/verifier.md against what the case studies say a brief and a verifier actually did.
- Try the method's own seam test on a codebase you already know: list every file each of several independent changes would touch, and see whether one file appears in several such lists at once.

VII. Prove it wrong

Cheapest first; the site-wide rules of engagement are stated once, on the Corrections page — not yet built, so this dossier does not link it.

- Count CASE-STUDY.md's own corrections yourself; a count that disagrees with the figure this dossier's Verified section quotes is a disproof.
- Read the worked example's timestamps against the commits it cites, and check each one exists.
- Apply the method to a real, multi-agent piece of work of your own and see whether the one failure mode still finds something the briefs missed; a clean round is evidence against the method's reach, not proof it is wrong, since “found nothing” is a stated, acceptable result here too.
