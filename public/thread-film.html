Bare beginnings, a shared darkroom, and a pipeline that outgrew the room it was built in.

IThe account

The earliest work here is bare, by Logan's own account: CanonBracketTool, A python based webtool and sdk wrapper for interacting with the Canon SDK to create shot sequences easily and automate shooting. (GitHub snapshot 2026-09-29), and Microscope-Stacker, A tool for stacking images captured on microscopes at various magnifications and exporting a final combined photo retaining detail at all magnifications in one image. (GitHub snapshot 2026-09-29).

atlas-darkroom grew out of the same cloud atlas as the Determinism thread's PrettyCloud: A print engine for the Atlas of Mathematical Forms (atlas-darkroom c1b9b6e README.md:3, private for now). It is private (GitHub snapshot 2026-09-29, private for now) for now. Extracted from it with their history carried: atlas-optical, The glass of the Eidograph, on its own (atlas-optical dbccafe README.md:3, private for now) (private (GitHub snapshot 2026-09-29, private for now) for now); and atlas-film, The medium of the Eidograph (atlas-film be1d674 README.md:3).

atlas-film underlies Quantum-Film, Film stocks whose crystals are laid by quantum circuits (Quantum-Film a7f02db README.md:3), checked at 14 (Quantum-Film a7f02db verify/run.sh) runner stages — the prints on Home are its output.

StoryDocs, a book-and-paper pipeline built from Markdown manifests (StoryDocs 673148c README.md:3, private for now: “Markdown manifests”), was taken out of atlas-darkroom (StoryDocs 673148c README.md:8, private for now) — the book side is the pipeline the Eidograph volumes were made with; the paper side, new, is a hybrid of a research paper and that book's own idiom. It is private (GitHub snapshot 2026-09-29, private for now) for now, born 2026-09-20 (StoryDocs 673148c git log, private for now). It has since touched half of the map's other projects, which the arrows out of it show, derived from its projects/ directories rather than typed by hand.
