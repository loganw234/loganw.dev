cft-fp256's contract, in its own words: “It is written so that an implementation can be built and proven without this repository's RTL or library - a different chip, a different process, a different language - and still be interchangeable with them bit for bit (cft-fp256 19de866 CONFORMANCE.md:9).” The checks below are how a reader confirms that directly, without taking this site's word for it, cheapest first.

IWays to check this

Don't trust this - run one.

  1. cft-fp256's own conformance replay, in the browser. Cost: a browser tab, nothing installed. It replays the published conformance vectors in front of you (cft-fp256 19de866 README.md:30).
  2. make golden in a clone of cft-fp256. Cost: 2.6 min at four workers (cft-fp256 19de866 docs/VERIFICATION.md:354) when quiet, 8 to 13 min (cft-fp256 19de866 docs/VERIFICATION.md:354) when loaded. The golden model's self-tests (cft-fp256 19de866 README.md:326): Its pytest suite checks the model against its own invariants and against mpmath and MPFR where they can arbitrate (cft-fp256 19de866 docs/VERIFICATION.md:37). Nothing below re-litigates a value it has decided (cft-fp256 19de866 docs/VERIFICATION.md:39).
  3. make verify-quick. Cost: ~20 min, 31 of 53 stages (cft-fp256 19de866 CLAUDE.md:9). `quick` is the docs, generated, buildargs, sweepjudge, innerskips and ensurevectors checks, the model-versus-C stages, the language's compiler (lang, about two and a half minutes run directly on a quiet desktop since its routines' legs left it on 2026-10-02), the GPU's photograph, the bindings, the language legs, the soak spot check, the workloads, the demos and the remote backend - about twenty minutes after a host build (cft-fp256 19de866 docs/VERIFICATION.md:404); it does not run what make verify-gate adds: the golden suite, the vectors, the library replay, the transcendentals, MPFR, the C++ replay, lint, formal, the C auditor's gate (audit, about four minutes since 2026-09-29), two cases of the estimates study (estimates, under a minute since 2026-09-30), the compiler's routines (lang-routines, about two and a half minutes run directly on a quiet desktop since 2026-10-02, and about two fifths more on the desktop with M1's build, item 5's legs for the six - 42 and 46 per cent of the old legs' time in two runs - and two thirds more on amd-arc-box, 252 s against 150 s, 245 s in its gate), the language's variational equations (tangent, three to six minutes since 2026-10-01, far longer on a busy desktop, and 9 to 19 per cent more with item 5's leg N on the desktop, a tenth more on amd-arc-box, 382 s against 346 s, 371 s in its gate) and the acceptance set (acceptance, about six minutes since 2026-10-02) and the stress driver's own suite (cardstress, about ten minutes in WSL since 2026-10-22; skipped by name while another process uses the card) (cft-fp256 19de866 docs/VERIFICATION.md:411). The gate: ~2 h quiet, 45 of 53 - what a change should pass (cft-fp256 19de866 CLAUDE.md:10); the document's own figure is “about two hours quiet and four loaded on this host (cft-fp256 19de866 docs/VERIFICATION.md:425)”. And, CI's green tick does not cover synthesis, timing or silicon (cft-fp256 19de866 README.md:319).
  4. python site/build.py --verify-facts in a clone of this site. Cost: one read per figure this site prints, at its pin or from the committed GitHub snapshot; a private repository's read is skipped by name. It proves every figure this site prints - including every quote above - is still exactly what its source gives right now; it does not prove that source's own claim is true, and a stated word has no source to re-read at all.

IIWhat checking here does not do

Every quotation above is read from cft-fp256's own documents, at the commit this site pins - the same discipline this whole site holds itself to. That shows this page quotes cft-fp256 faithfully. It does not show that cft-fp256's claims are true: cft-fp256's own gates decide that, and the checks above are how a reader runs them directly, rather than taking this site's word for it.

What cft-fp256's own documents say their checks do not reach:

  • “One stage in this layer holds the library to something this project did not compute (photograph): atlas-engine's deterministic camera around a plate as a sequencer program, each pass's deposit buffer held to the SHA-256 an NVIDIA GPU wrote while rendering the same frame from pinned GLSL. Every other stage here compares the C with the golden model, and a defect the two shared would pass them all; this one would not (cft-fp256 19de866 docs/VERIFICATION.md:87).” The record shows a defect of that kind: “Under rmm IEEE 754's roundTiesToAway gives sigma; transcend.exp2 gave +0 (cft-fp256 19de866 docs/VALIDATION.md:18209),” and “golden, libcft and the oracle agree at ba03b1d, and at 3a2b2d0 exactly the four rmm ties are wrong (cft-fp256 19de866 docs/VALIDATION.md:18222).” Step 6's design study M1 found it (its question 5), verifier-VM1 confirmed it (cft-fp256 19de866 CONFORMANCE.md:402), in the entry 2026-10-05 - the golden model's exp2 tie fixed, and the conformance profile's major step to 3 (parcel XT); M1's design study merged as a design record (cft-fp256 19de866 docs/VALIDATION.md:18206).
  • “no gate compares a set's digest with vectors/SHA256SUMS (cft-fp256 19de866 CONFORMANCE.md:316).” The same passage goes on: “A change that moves a recorded bit or case - to the model and libcft together, or to the generator alone - therefore passes the gate budget at the vector sets (cft-fp256 19de866 CONFORMANCE.md:320).”
