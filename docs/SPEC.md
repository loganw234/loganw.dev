## 2. Decisions (Logan, 2026-09-29)

Each decision is given in Logan's words where they were brief, and summarised
where they were longer.

**Structure and hosting**

1. **The repository:** public at `loganw234/loganw.dev`, with an MIT licence.
2. **Hosting:** GitHub Pages at the domain loganw.dev. Logan updates the DNS.
3. **Where the build runs:** on the desktop, which has every clone. The output
   is committed along with a manifest of pins and hashes. CI re-checks
   whatever the public repositories allow, and names each figure it can't
   check.
4. **Pins:** each pin moves by hand, as a dated commit. A scheduled job may
   report how far a pin trails its repository, but nothing moves a pin
   automatically.
5. **Private repositories:** any fact may be drawn from them. They will go
   public eventually, and their facts will switch to the checkable kind then.
   Until that happens, the site marks them *private for now*.
6. **Fonts:** served from the site itself. All eight files were downloaded
   at Logan's word: Newsreader and JetBrains Mono, woff2 in Latin and
   extended Latin, plus the two OFL licence texts, 512,869 bytes in all
   (`site/fonts/SOURCES.txt`).
7. **Deploying:** every push to main deploys, from the start.

**Content**

8. **The ratio.** It will be measured from now on. In Logan's words, the
   human-active time reported so far "was a bit inflated, lining up more with
   the 1:24 figure". Until measurements exist, 1:24 is Logan's statement and
   is marked as stated.
9. **Co-authorship.** "AI Collaborators are attributed equal to the human
   one, the projects wouldnt exist if either were removed."
10. **Other contributors** (the Preservation thread's): credited directly,
    with links back.
11. **The Home ledger column is called "last verified".** It is defined in
    section 4.
12. **The Record page.** Only three repositories keep a ledger, so the Record
    is weighted towards those. This is accepted "as how the method grew".
13. **binary-sites and StoryDocs** will be made public and added like the
    other projects. **nextpnr-xilinx** (the fork) and **Microscope-Stacker**
    are listed but not explained in depth.
14. **The biography:** Logan will approve a draft. The contact address is
    `logan@loganw.dev` "for now".
15. **The first version** gives full dossiers to cft-fp256, Quantum-Film,
    cft-rebound, and the two methods (HonestFramework and ParcelRound).
16. **Wally** is Logan's Discord username. It appears only on the game
    thread's page.
17. **Project order** (Logan's account): PrettyCloud was the first project and
    grew into the Atlas family, then cft-fp256, then the projects built on it.
    The earliest projects, CanonBracketTool and Microscope-Stacker, were "my
    beginning projects" and "especially bare". The mercs2 work was "more
    involved with AI and the systems Im using now".

**How the work runs**

## 4. Definitions the build uses

- **Last verified.** The newest recorded pass of a repository's own gate, at
  or before its pin. Two kinds of record count, and each repository declares
  which it uses in `pins.json` under `verified_by`:
  - a run of a named CI workflow that succeeded on the pinned commit or an
    ancestor of it;
  - a ledger line matching a declared pattern, dated by the entry it sits in.

  A repository that declares neither shows a dash, and the dash says why.
  Deploy and release workflows don't count as verification, so they are
  never declared.
- **Open regressions.** The newest finished run, at or before the pin, of
  each declared workflow that did not pass. When every declared workflow
  passed, the site records that too, as an absence.
- **Agent-written.** The number of commits carrying a Claude or Gemini
  Co-Authored-By trailer, out of all commits at the pin. It counts commits,
  not trailer lines and not lines of code. A fork gets a dash.
- **Born.** A repository's earliest commit at its pin. The exceptions:
  - a repository split out of another with its history carried is born on
    the date its README states;
  - a repository read only through the snapshot is born on the snapshot's
    earliest commit date.
- **Private for now.** A figure from a private repository is printed with its
  source, which the reader can't open yet. That a repository is private is
  itself only the snapshot's word, because a stranger sees a private
  repository and a missing one the same way.
- **Stated.** Logan's word, with no file behind it. It is printed as stated,
  and it is never counted as sourced.
